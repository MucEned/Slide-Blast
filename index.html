How to play

  • Drag a row sideways or a column up and down. Gems pushed off one edge come back on the other side. Slides are unlimited.
  • Arrange gems of one color into the target shape above the board. The moment your slide completes it, it blasts by itself. Rotated and mirrored shapes count.
  • If falling gems happen to form the shape, it glows green but waits for you: tap it to take it.
  • The group must match the shape exactly. If an extra gem of the same color touches it, it won't blast, so slide that gem away first.
  • The game is endless. You start with 30 seconds, and every finished shape adds time and brings a new shape. Slides are unlimited.
  • The game ends when the clock hits zero.
  • The higher your score, the harder it gets: bigger shapes appear, up to 2 new gem colors join, and each shape adds less time (3 s, then 2.5 s, then 2 s).
  • Break: tap a clump of 5 or more same-color gems that isn't a target to clear it out of your way. It scores nothing, adds no time, and recharges for 10 seconds (the dial next to Swap).
  • Don't like the shape? Press Swap next to it to get a different one. The button recharges for 10 seconds and counts down on its face.
  • Combo: finish the next shape within 3 seconds of the board settling and the combo grows. Each shape scores 100 per gem × your combo.
