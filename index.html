How to play

  • Drag a row sideways or a column up and down. Gems pushed off one edge come back on the other side. Slides are unlimited.
  • Arrange gems of one color into the target shape at the top. The moment your slide completes it, it blasts by itself. Rotated and mirrored shapes count.
  • If falling gems happen to form the shape, it glows green but waits for you: tap it to take it.
  • The group must match the shape exactly. If an extra gem of the same color touches it, it won't blast, so slide that gem away first.
  • The game is endless. You start with 60 seconds, and every finished shape adds time and brings a new shape. The game ends when the clock hits zero.
  • The higher your score, the harder it gets: bigger and trickier shapes appear (up to 6 gems), up to 2 new gem colors join, and each shape adds less time (3 s, then 2.5 s, then 2 s).
  • Explode: drag the bomb from the bottom right onto any group of same-color gems. The group blows up and scores 100 per gem, so bigger groups are worth more. It adds no time and recharges for 10 seconds.
  • Special gems sometimes fall in: a bomb gem glows orange with embers circling it; when it blasts, the 8 gems around it go too, and a bomb caught in that blast goes off as well. An electric gem crackles with blue lightning; when it blasts, every gem of its color on the board goes with it. Every gem an effect destroys scores extra points.
  • Double: if two or more groups match the target at the same moment, they all blast together and that blast scores ×3 points.
  • Combo: finish the next shape within 3 seconds of the board settling and the combo grows. Each shape scores 100 per gem × your combo.
