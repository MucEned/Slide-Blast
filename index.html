Slide & Blast

Level 1
Score 0
Time 30.0

How to play

  • Drag a row sideways or a column up and down. Gems pushed off one edge come back on the other side. Slides are unlimited.
  • Arrange gems of one color into one of the target shapes shown above the board, then tap the group to blast it. Rotated and mirrored shapes count.
  • The group must match the shape exactly. If an extra gem of the same color touches it, it won't blast, so slide that gem away first.
  • Build every target within 30 seconds. The clock starts on your first move, and each second left over pays 20 points.
