Slide & Blast

Score 0
Combo ×1
Time 30.0

How to play

  • Drag a row sideways or a column up and down. Gems pushed off one edge come back on the other side. Slides are unlimited.
  • Arrange gems of one color into one of the 3 target shapes above the board, then tap the group to blast it. Rotated and mirrored shapes count.
  • The group must match the shape exactly. If an extra gem of the same color touches it, it won't blast, so slide that gem away first.
  • The game is endless. You start with 30 seconds, and every finished shape adds +3 s and brings a new shape. The game ends when the clock hits zero.
  • Don't like the shapes? Press Swap next to them to get 3 new ones. The button recharges for 10 seconds and counts down on its face.
  • Combo: finish the next shape within 3 seconds of the board settling and the combo grows. Each shape scores 100 per gem × your combo.
